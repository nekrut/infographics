\sect{Breadth of applications}
Galaxy spans genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics, single cell, variant analysis, genome assembly, epigenetics, metagenomics, microbiome, machine learning, imaging, climate science, ecology, evolution, computational chemistry, materials science, synthetic biology, FAIR data, and One Health.

\sect{Funding}

\sect{Global infrastructure}
The \textbf{usegalaxy.*} consortium provides free public servers:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{usegalaxy.org} -- USA (ACCESS-CI)
  \item \textbf{usegalaxy.eu} -- Europe (de.NBI / ELIXIR)
  \item \textbf{usegalaxy.org.au} -- Australia (BioCommons)
\end{itemize}
{\small\color{textgray} Every user: 250\,GB storage, 1\,TB scratch, 8 concurrent jobs -- free.}

\columnbreak

\sect{Galaxy Training Network}
The world's largest open bioinformatics training resource:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{489 tutorials} across 35 topics
  \item \textbf{214 training videos}, \textbf{499 contributors}
  \item Galaxy Training Academy 2025: \textbf{3,700+ simultaneous participants}
  \item Annual Galaxy Community Conference
  \item Peer-reviewed, community-driven, free and open
\end{itemize}

\sect{Major international initiatives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{AnVIL} -- NHGRI's genomic data science platform
  \item \textbf{BRC Analytics} -- pathogen, host, and vector genomics
  \item \textbf{CFDE} -- NIH Common Fund Data Ecosystem
  \item \textbf{VGP} -- Vertebrate Genomes Project (600 assemblies)
\end{itemize}
