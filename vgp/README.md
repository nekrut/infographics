# VGP on Galaxy

An interactive slideshow presenting the Vertebrate Genomes Project (VGP) on Galaxy - building reference-quality genomes for 70,000 vertebrate species.

## View Online

**https://nekrut.github.io/infographics/vgp/**

## Contents

9 slides covering:

1. **VGP Overview** - Key statistics (2,330 workflows, 461K core-hours)
2. **Building the Tree of Life** - VGP mission and goals
3. **Assembly Pipeline** - 10 modular workflows across 5 stages
4. **VGP Workflows** - Wordcloud of assembly tools and workflows
5. **Computational Scale** - Runtime, jobs, memory statistics
6. **3 Years of Genome Assembly** - Timeline July 2022 - October 2025
7. **Galaxy + ACCESS-CI** - Infrastructure powering VGP
8. **Key Resources** - Links to VGP Galaxy, GenomeArk, etc.
9. **QR Code** - Scan to visit vgp.usegalaxy.org

## Data Source

Statistics derived from `vgp_workflows_assembly_runs_metrics.json.gz` containing 2,330 workflow invocations and 40,600+ job metrics.
